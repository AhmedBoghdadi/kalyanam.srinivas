About the academy

Practical creative education rooted in Telugu language, culture and modern AI filmmaking workflows.

Why this academy

Learn the tools, keep the story human.

Kalyanam Srinivas AI Film Making Academy brings together Telugu-first education and a creative studio perspective. The emphasis is on understanding a workflow: from an idea and a prompt to video, music, voice, visual effects and a finished story.

Business details, schedules and creative credits should always be confirmed directly with the academy.

Our working principles

  • Telugu-first explanation and context
  • Practical, project-led learning
  • Responsible use of AI tools
  • No unsupported promises
